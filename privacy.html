Privacy policy

Frappe Backup is a private backup tool used only by its owner.

What it accesses

Only files it creates itself in the owner's Google Drive (Google's drive.file permission). It cannot see or open any other files.

What it does with that access

It uploads encrypted backup files of the owner's own Frappe site, checks that they uploaded correctly, and deletes old backups after 30 days. The backups are encrypted before they leave the owner's computer.

Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to or sent to anyone else. There is no analytics or tracking. Frappe Backup's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the "Frappe Backups" folder in Google Drive deletes all stored data.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for Frappe Backup.
